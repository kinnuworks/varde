Privacy + AODA

Greentec is certified and complies with data protection regulations.

Greentec International Inc. (“Greentec”) respects your right to privacy and complies with applicable privacy laws.  This privacy policy describes how we collect, use, process and share your personal information when you use our website or otherwise provide information directly to us. 

This privacy policy does not describe how we process personal information that may come into our possession indirectly via our business clients in the course of providing them with services, as such processing is governed by the terms of the specific contracts we have with our clients. 

 

What is Personal Information?

Personal Information is generally considered to include any information related to an identifiable individual, and any information specifically defined as “personally identifiable information”, “personal information” and “personal data” or similar terms under applicable privacy laws. 

 

What Personal Information Do We Collect?

We may collect personal information directly from you when you interact with our website, though electronic communications, or when you interact with us on the telephone or in person. For example, you may provide us with your name, job title, contact information and other information when you: 

  • Set up an account with us
  • Provide information to us in connection with the purchase of our services
  • Subscribe to our newsletter and blogs
  • Contact us by email, text, phone or in person for client support or other matters

We limit the information that we collect to that necessary for conducting business.

 

Automatic Collection

We may also collect personal information automatically via cookies and similar technologies. 

 

How Do We Use Your Personal Information?

We use and process your personal information to provide, improve and customize our services, to develop new services, and to send you communications and comply with legal requirements. By providing your information and/or using our services with knowledge of how we collect, use and disclose your information, you consent to the collection, use and disclosure of your information as described in this policy. Your personal information is used to: 

  • Provide our services under a contractual obligation to you or your company
  • Create and manage your account and authenticate your identity
  • Send you forms and/or invoices
  • Provide you with customer support and respond to your inquiries
  • As permitted by applicable laws, send communications to you including commercial electronic messages and promotional communications (which you may opt out of)
  • When you visit our website, collect information relating to your use of our website
  • Develop our services
  • Protect and ensure the security of our systems and services
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Otherwise as required or permitted by applicable laws


Who Do We Share Your Personal Information With?

We may share your personal information with our third party service providers and contractors to carry out our business activities such as sending marketing or other communications, billing and payment processing, IT and security services and service improvement initiatives. 

In accordance with applicable laws, we may also share your information in connection with a business transaction such as a merger, sale of assets, restructuring, financing or acquisition of all or part of our business. 

We require, by contract, that any person or organization providing products or services to Greentec on our behalf will protect the confidentiality of such information.

We do not sell personal information. We do not provide this information to persons or organizations outside our organization for their marketing or business development purposes.

Any information that we receive is kept confidential. Our employees who have access to information are made aware that information is to be kept confidential. Each employee must certify that he or she has maintained the confidentiality of the information entrusted to him or her.

 

How Do We Protect Your Personal Information?

Greentec takes every precaution necessary to protect your information and uses physical, organizational and technological safeguards. While no safeguards can be considered 100% secure, we have adopted stringent measures to safeguard your information and have adopted industry standard security measures.

 

How Do We Retain and Dispose of Personal Information?

We retain your personal information as needed to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected and will thereafter take steps to delete, destroy or anonymise your personal information as required by applicable laws.

 

What Choices Do I Have Regarding My Personal Information?

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have certain rights with respect to your personal information including rights of access and rectification. 

Please be advised that Greentec acts as a data processor/service provider with respect to our business clients’ data (including personal information held by our clients) on behalf of clients under the terms of our services agreement. Such personal information is under the control of our client and not under the control of Greentec. If you make a request regarding your personal information that is under the control of our business client, we will direct such request to our client and will assist our client to respond to your request in accordance with our agreement with them. 

For personal information we directly collect, you have the right to ask that your personal information be corrected, to access the personal information we have about you, and to withdraw your consent to our further collection, use and disclosure of your personal information, subject to legal and contractual restrictions. You may also have other rights such as deletion/erasure and the right to request and obtain your personal data in a structured, commonly used format,

 

Electronic Marketing

In compliance with CASL, all commercial electronic messages sent by us will include an unsubscribe mechanism, allowing you to opt out of future communications. Unsubscribe requests will be processed promptly, and in any event, within 10 business days.

 

How Do We Address Changes to Our Privacy Policy?

We reserve the right to modify this privacy policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and your continued use of the website after such changes have been posted will constitute your acceptance of the revised privacy policy.
