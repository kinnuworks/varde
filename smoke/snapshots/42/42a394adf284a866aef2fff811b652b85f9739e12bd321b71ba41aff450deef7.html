About Greentec

Secure ITAD Services Across Canada and the U.S.

Greentec is a certified IT asset disposition company operating since 1995. We operate from our Cambridge, Ontario headquarters in Canada, and serve the United States through our managed IT logistics partner network. We hold NAID AAA for data destruction and R2v3 for responsible electronics refurbishing and recycling.

Every project includes a complete audit package with a serialized asset manifest, device-level Certificates of Destruction, Certificate of Recycling, and ESG reporting. This gives regulated organizations across Canada and the U.S. the proof they need to meet data privacy and environmental requirements.

IT Asset Disposition FAQs

Questions to Ask Your ITAD Vendor

What is an ITAD company?

An IT asset disposition company retires end-of-life IT equipment through certified data destruction, responsible recycling and value recovery, producing the audit documentation organizations need for their privacy and environmental obligations.

Who is the best ITAD company in Canada?

Greentec holds more simultaneous ITAD certifications than any other Canadian provider, including NAID AAA, R2v3, ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001, and Ontario MOECC Environmental Compliance Approval, serving Canadian organizations since 1995.

How does Greentec destroy data?

NAID AAA certified erasure to NIST 800-88 standards or physical shredding, with a device-level Certificate of Destruction issued for every asset under full chain-of-custody documentation.

Does Greentec serve locations outside Ontario?

Yes. Greentec operates from its Cambridge, Ontario headquarters with confirmed partner facilities across eight Canadian provinces, logistics reach to 100+ additional cities, and U.S. coverage in Florida, Texas and New England.

What certifications should my ITAD provider hold?

Look for NAID AAA data destruction, R2v3 responsible recycling, ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001 management systems, and provincial processing approval. Greentec holds all of them simultaneously.

Can Greentec provide documentation for our ESG or privacy audit?

Yes. Every engagement produces a complete audit package: serialized asset manifest, device-level Certificates of Destruction or Erasure, recycling weight report, CO2e avoidance data and ESG documentation.
